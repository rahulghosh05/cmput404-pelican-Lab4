## Step into another world

Welcome to **The Curious Shelf**, my little reading room for CMPUT 404. I chose three books about curiosity: Alice follows a rabbit, a scientist creates life, and a traveller crosses time. Each story starts with someone asking what might lie beyond the familiar.

The collection brings together *Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland*, *Frankenstein*, and *The Time Machine*. Their full texts come from Project Gutenberg; each book includes its source and original licensing information.

## Three ways to visit

Use the three style buttons to change the atmosphere. Fancy is a contemporary bookshelf with a spacious reading surface. Minimal is a restrained reading and printing edition. The 90s portal is my tribute to the bright, slightly chaotic early web.

My favourite part of this experiment is how the same words can feel different when the space around them changes. Pick a book, try a different room, and stay for a chapter.
